Assumptions & open points

  • Approvals are Owner-owned: both the first approval (after the quotation is attached) and the final fund release come from the Owner (Gary), not an Approver. The scoped Approver role no longer gates this flow.
  • NUS bank account branch: "Have you registered your bank account with NUS before? = Yes" skips document collection; "No" issues a doc request back to the requester for the vendor-creation form + bank-account proof.
  • Vendor creation (Coordinator): once all docs are submitted and in order, Coordinator — admin staff, not an Approver role — manually registers the vendor under OFN before the flow continues to the invoice step.
  • Value check: the requester submits the invoice / keys in the value; if it matches or is below the quoted / approved amount it proceeds, otherwise it is queried and resubmitted. Initially a manual gate.
  • BMC Helix RFP: the separate Finance Ops RFP + OFN payment-approval steps are replaced by a single BMC Helix RFP block. It sends a callback once approved by OFN, after which the Owner (Gary) gives one final approval to release the funds.
  • Final release outcomes: if the Owner does not release the funds, there are three paths — resubmit with a different invoice / approved claim amount (back to the value step), rejected (terminal state), or re-request for approval (starts over from the top).
  • BMC Helix automation: future work — the processed payment hands off to BMC Helix for payment automation.
