Assumptions & open points

  • Finance Ops moved downstream: Finance Ops no longer does manual processing right after approval — they enter only to submit the Request for Payment (RFP) once the invoice value has cleared the check.
  • Payment account branch: "Payment account on file = Yes" skips document collection; "No" issues a doc request back to the requester for the vendor-creation form + bank-account proof before continuing to the invoice step.
  • Value check: the requester submits the invoice / keys in the value; if it matches or is below the quoted / approved amount it proceeds, otherwise it is queried and resubmitted. Initially a manual gate.
  • Payment approval: after Finance Ops submits the RFP, the Finance Office routes it to the Approver for payment approval; on approval the payment is processed.
  • Payment platform: future work — the processed payment hands off to the payment platform for automation.
