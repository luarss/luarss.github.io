Projects & Publications

Open source projects and research publications.

I actively contribute to open source projects in AI agents and AI-assisted tools for chip design. My work spans from production AI agent runtimes to MCP servers for developer tooling.

Publications & Writing#

OpenROAD MCP: Let AI Close Your Timing (Feb 2026) Introducing OpenROAD MCP — a server that enables AI assistants like Claude Code to interact directly with OpenROAD processes for timing analysis and constraint adjustment.

Building Robust AI Research Agents: A tale of five specialists (Nov 2025) Four architectural iterations of a deep research workflow for VCs — from a single agent to five specialised agents with hierarchical citations, MCP integration, and iterative refinement.

Multi-Agent Systems Are 23x Cheaper Than Deep Research Models with Higher Quality (Nov 2025) Benchmarked multi-agent systems (11 specialised agents) against single-model and deep reasoning models across 5 scientific domains. Multi-agent won or tied in all 5 at 23× lower cost.

ORAssistant: A Custom RAG-based Conversational Assistant for OpenROAD (Oct 2024) Paper on a custom RAG-based conversational assistant for chip design automation using OpenROAD.

Extended Tactile Perception: Vibration Sensing through Tools and Grasped Objects (IROS 2021 — Best Paper Award) ML classification models for high-dimensional time-series tactile sensor data enabling vibration sensing through tools and grasped objects.
