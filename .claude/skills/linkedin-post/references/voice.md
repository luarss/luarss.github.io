# Voice

## Who I am

- **Name:** Shui Song Luar
- **Role:** Applied AI engineer leading a team at NUSX, the technology transfer arm of NUS
- **Writing for:** engineers who might join the team, and colleagues and leaders in Singapore tech and university circles
- **Not writing for:** only cybersecurity specialists

## What I sound like

- Plain English, short sentences, contractions.
- First person. States what happened and what the team is building.
- No emoji, no bold labels, no contrarian posturing.
- A timeline or arc in the first lines ("Six months ago I joined... Today I lead the team").
- Lists of concrete things being built, each with a visible outcome.
- Ends with a specific ask and the role bar, then one line of invitation.

## Post that worked

```
Six months ago I joined NUSX to fix one problem.

Today I lead the team working on it.

Here's what happened:

NUS has thousands of inventions and patents on record. Behind every one is a trail of inboxes, spreadsheets and systems that don't talk to each other.

We're teaching AI to automate the boring stuff.

What we are cooking:

• A data lake of hundreds of tables, refreshed by automated pipelines, so everything below runs on current data.

• A natural language layer over our tech transfer data.

• An end-to-end contract agent that drafts agreements for review and signature, and links to payment.

• A screening agent that reviews applications, so people spend their time on the promising ones.

• Enterprise AI enablement, so colleagues who don't code can build and run their own agents and workflows.

• One brain across every system in the tech transfer office. Agents read from all of them, act on what they find, and write back under guardrails enforced in code, not in the prompt.

We're hiring an Applied AI Engineer to build this with us.

You'll need 3+ years building software or integrations, and you've shipped production grade agents that calls an LLM API.

If that sounds like you, let's talk.

Apply: https://lnkd.in/gKCPjFYk

#hiring #AIEngineer #AppliedAI #NUSX #NUS #Singapore
```

Why it worked:

- Personal arc and time contrast in the opener.
- A pain almost every reader has felt: inboxes and spreadsheets that don't talk.
- Six concrete items in plain words.
- A clear role, a clear bar and a clear ask.

## Post that did not work

The CyberSG Atlas chatbot post, adapted from the blog write-up.

Why it did not work:

- Project report, no personal stake.
- Niche pain (SMEs choosing between MDR, GRC and VAPT).
- Jargon: BM25, Shadow DOM, RAG, prompt injection, red-teaming.
- No call to action.
- Probably a link to the blog in the body.

Strongest unused hooks from that write-up:

- The model claimed to be "a local AI model" that sent nothing to Google. It wasn't.
- The model included required safety notices on one run and dropped them on the next, so code now adds them.
- 68 red-team prompts found five gaps before launch.

## Positions

1. Guarantees belong in code, not in the prompt.
2. Test against the live model, and read the logs after launch.
3. Keep scope small and set spend limits before launch.

## Off limits

- Contact details, emails and phone numbers of any person.
- Unreleased NUSX contract, pricing or applicant data.
- Claims about hiring outcomes, headcount or budget that are not in the source.

## Proof I can use

Only what is in the source. Examples already public: the CyberSG Atlas launch on 10 November 2026 with 200+ listed companies, 68 red-team prompts, five gaps found and fixed.
