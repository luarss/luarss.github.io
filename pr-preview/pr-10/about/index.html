I’m an AI & Systems Engineer at NUSX Labs, part of NUSX. I build LLM systems and agents, and the cloud infrastructure they run on. I usually own an internal platform end to end, from design through to production and daily use. Recent work includes a claims approval system, our data pipelines, an LLM evaluation harness, and zero-trust access across our services.

Previously I was a Founding Engineer at SoraNova, an AI Engineer at Espressif Systems, and a Graduate Research Assistant at NUS where I published a Best Paper Award-winning paper at IROS 2021.
